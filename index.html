        Help

        How does it work?Every hour is its own market. Buy an hour, and when it starts you meet the provider for one hour. Both of you tap Check in.

        What is $CLOCK?In this preview it is play money used to pay for hours, put down deposits and pay small fees.

        Why do prices move?They are simulated, and your own trades also move them.

        What if the provider does not show?You are refunded plus a bonus from their deposit.

        What if I forget to check in?You get no refund. Providers who forget lose their deposit.

        Can I sell an hour back?Yes, any time before it starts.

        How do I keep my account?Open your wallet and tap Back up. Restore it on another device from the same page.
