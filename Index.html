        Terms and privacy

        Help

        How does it work?Every hour is its own market. Buy an hour, and when it starts you meet the provider for one hour. Both of you tap Check in.

        Where do prices come from?From real offers and bids by other people. If nobody has traded an hour yet, there is no price until someone offers or bids.

        What if the provider does not show?You are refunded and receive their deposit as a bonus.

        What if I forget to check in?You get no refund. Providers who forget lose their deposit.

        Can I sell an hour back?Yes, any time before it starts.

        How do I use my account on another device?Open your wallet, copy your login key, and choose “I already have a login key” on the other device.
